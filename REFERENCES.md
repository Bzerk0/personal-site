# References

Captured on 2026-11-13 with a headless browser. Screenshots are in `screenshots/references/`.
These are used for mood and interaction quality only. No code, assets, layouts, logos, or branding were copied.

## sadok.mx

Screenshots: `sadok-1-loading.png`, `sadok-2-loaded.png`

What it does:
- A black screen with a small pixel-font `LOADING.. 100%` counter in the center.
- After loading, a retro handheld sits in the middle with two short instructions next to it: click to power on, or press any key.
- The owner's name and roles sit quietly in the top-left corner the whole time.

What I took from it:
- The order of events: real loading counter, then one clear "press to start" moment, then the thing powers on.
- Click and keyboard both work for every step.
- Whose site it is stays visible in a corner at all times (my top-left "Hudson Bjerkness").
- A plain route for people who don't want the toy (my "Skip to site" link and the Portfolio page).

What I did not take: the handheld console, the starfield, the rainbow road, the fonts.

## Flynn's Arcade, Tron (1982)

Screenshot: `flynns-arcade-still.png`

What it shows:
- Almost the whole frame is black. The sign is the only real light source.
- The glow lights the brick right around the sign and falls off quickly.
- The doorways below are dark shapes.

What I took from it:
- Darkness first: the wall is only visible where the sign lights it.
- Brick texture that shows up in the glow.
- Warm light at the doors against the cold dark.

What I did not take: the sign itself. Theirs is red block capitals with layered outline tubes on a curved wall above arches. Mine is a single-stroke script-style font in magenta and cyan on a flat wall above glass doors.

## logartis.info

Screenshot: `logartis-1.png`

What it does:
- Four plain text links in the top-right corner, medium weight, generous even spacing, no boxes or icons.
- The menu never competes with the page title.

What I took from it:
- Top-right placement, text-only links, even spacing, quiet until you interact.
- On the flat pages my menu uses the same idea: clean sans-serif links with an underline that slides in.

What I could not capture: the hover and click behaviour. The first matching link in the page was a hidden phone-menu copy, so the automated hover timed out. My hover, focus, and current-page states are my own design.
